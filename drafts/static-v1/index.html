FMC
US Federal Maritime Commission. U.S. federal law (the Shipping Act of 1984) requires Ocean Transportation Intermediaries to be licensed and bonded, which protects consumers. Universal Relocations Inc., a New Jersey corporation, is licensed by the FMC under license no. FMC-OTI NF025083 to operate NVOCC services for international household goods shipments to and from the USA.
