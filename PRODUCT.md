## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript, plain CSS Modules plus a global token sheet, no Tailwind. Chosen for a deploy-ready Vercel target and full control over a bespoke visual system.

## Users

The audience the current universalrelocations.com already serves (user instruction: "what we have in the site"):

- Individuals and families moving internationally, with strong USA ↔ India and UAE corridors (the three hubs and their toll-free lines).
- Corporate expatriates and diplomats: the company's founding client base since 1998, many still clients today.
- Companies relocating staff or offices (corporate relocation, office relocation).
- People moving within or between US states (intrastate and interstate).

Their job: hand an anxious, high-stakes, multi-country move to one trusted partner and know what it will cost and when it will arrive.

## Product Purpose

Universal Relocations is a full-service moving and relocation company: survey, packing, freight (ocean, air, groupage), customs at both ends, storage, and delivery. Home page success is a **free quote request**; moving calculator, video pre-move survey, and hub phone calls are secondary paths.

## Positioning

"The ONE-STOP relocation service" (the logo tagline) for international, interstate and intrastate shipping. One company owns every handoff, with its own hubs in the USA, India and the UAE, and is an FMC-licensed NVOCC for household goods to and from the USA.

## Operating Context

Visitors are planning a move weeks or months out, often comparing movers, often across time zones (US / India / UAE). Real tools on the site: moving calculator (volume estimate + quote), video-call pre-move survey booking, talk to a move manager, file a claim, moving checklist, packing tips, moving/shipping terms, knowledge base, FAQ, blog.

## Product Principles

1. One partner, every handoff: show the whole move owned end to end.
2. Certainty before commitment: volume, cost and timeline visible early.
3. Local on both ends: three hubs, real phone lines, real time zones.
4. Proof over adjectives: licenses, ratings and real reviews do the persuading.
