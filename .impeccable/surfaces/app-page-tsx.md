# Home page (app/page.tsx)

Scope: Universal Relocations home page only, Next.js App Router. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: as PRODUCT.md (families/expats on USA–India–UAE corridors, corporate expats and diplomats, companies, US interstate movers).
Action: request a free quote, in working form in the first viewport. Secondary: moving calculator, video survey, call a hub.
Proof: since 1998, Trustpilot 4.5 (2,000+), Google 4.4, three real Google reviews, press logos, FMC/DOT licences.
Constraints: light theme; navy #00056E + orange #FEA70E/#FB8E28 pinned; no invented claims; quote submit has no backend yet (route handler returns a reference, flagged to the user).
Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a bill of lading, the document Universal actually issues as an FMC-licensed NVOCC. You request a quote by filling in its numbered boxes. It refuses the category default of a stock family-with-boxes photo hero and a floating quote card.

OWN-WORLD: A white bond sheet on a pale navy desk, built from 1px navy hairline boxes, each with a tiny numbered small-caps label at top-left. Two spot inks print the form: navy for print and reversed title bars, orange only for rubber stamps and the primary action, multiplied where stamps overprint hairlines and labels. A third, non-printed ink is the typewriter ribbon (#14161c), used only for typed entries: field values, references, licence numbers, the handled-by entry. Printed labels use Archivo (wide/condensed widths); typed entries use Courier Prime. States: Received, Surveyed, Packed, Cleared, Shipped, Delivered ("Shipped" replaced "Afloat" because freight can be air as well as ocean).

STORY: Visitors understand that one company owns every handoff, believe it from the licences, ratings and real reviews printed on the form, and fill in the quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Reversed navy licence strip, then the masthead (logo, nav, orange quote button). The sheet's title block holds a huge Archivo-expanded headline across 8 columns. To its right is an empty dashed "Received" stamp box. Below, boxes 01–07 (from, to, month, home size, name, email, phone) span full width, with the orange "Issue my free quote" action closing the row.

FORM: Bill of lading / customs paperwork, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key a28b339a.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: On submit, an orange distressed RECEIVED stamp thumps into the empty stamp box, overprinting the dashed rule and reference rows (scale/rotate/settle, sheet nudges 2px), and carries the date and a reference number. On phones the stamp lands in the action box the visitor just pressed. On scroll, the particulars table rows get stamped in sequence.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
