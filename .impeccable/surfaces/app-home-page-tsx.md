## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a route you can see. A navy daylight banner holds a slowly turning dotted globe with Universal's own corridors lit in orange, and the quote form sits in a floating planner whose map draws your route as you type it. It refuses the stock family-with-boxes photo hero and the paperwork costume of v2.

OWN-WORLD: Light ground tinted toward the brand navy (mist #f2f3f9, white panels), deep navy #00056E bands with soft blue glows, orange #FEA70E only for action pills, route arcs, live pins and emphasis words on navy. Soft geometry: 40px banners, 24px panels, pill buttons whose round navy chip carries the arrow. Archivo across its width axis for everything; JetBrains Mono only for live data (hub clocks, route codes, references). Dots are the material: land as dot fields, offices as pulsing points, routes as arcs.

STORY: Visitors see their own corridor drawn by one company end to end, believe it from the hubs, licences, ratings and reviews, and send the two-step quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A rounded navy banner inset 20px from the window edge, with the nav inside it (reversed logo, links, toll-free number, orange quote pill). Two columns on desktop. On the left: a left-aligned three-line Archivo headline, "One company for every mile of your move", with "every mile" in orange, then a lede, an orange quote pill plus a ghost calculator pill, and a one-line ratings row. On the right: a large interactive dotted globe (cobe, in brand blues; a user request after the Rivals landing globe), with orange office markers, the three hub corridors as orange arcs and labelled hubs. The white planner panel overlaps the banner bottom: a dot map with the route on the left (about 58%) and step one of the quote form on the right, its first field visible at 1440x900.

FORM: Pinned reference translation (Stable Auto layout, Universal palette and content); no concept roll, since a user-pinned direction beats the roll.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Typing an origin and destination redraws the planner arc between the two pins. The arc grows and a pulse travels it, and the leg list updates with the place names. The same dotted globe recurs in the hubs band, where it turns to face the hub you pick, and rises behind the closing CTA.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
